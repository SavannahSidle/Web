Common questions

Before a project begins.

What does a small business website cost in Canada?

MICA scopes each project individually. The quote reflects the pages, content, interactions, and custom functionality the business actually needs, with a clear scope established before work begins.

How long does a custom website take?

The schedule depends on the project’s scope and how ready the content is. MICA confirms the timeline with the agreed scope rather than promising a generic turnaround.

Does MICA Digital work with businesses outside Ottawa?

Yes. MICA is based in Ottawa, and works remotely with businesses across Ontario and Canada.

What is accessible web design?

Accessible web design considers how people with different sensory, physical, and cognitive needs understand and use a website. It includes clear structure, readable content, keyboard access, appropriate contrast, and predictable interaction.

What is mobile-first web design?

Mobile-first design begins with the essential content and actions on smaller screens, then expands them for tablets and computers. MICA builds responsive websites intended to work clearly across devices.

Can MICA redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign can preserve useful content and brand recognition while improving structure, credibility, accessibility, performance, and the path to contact or purchase.

What is included in a MICA website project?

The exact scope varies, but projects can include information architecture, content support, custom visual design, responsive development, accessibility foundations, testing, and a practical handoff with ownership of the finished website.
