Purpose before complexity

Use technology where it earns its place.

Web development should support the content, interaction, and business process rather than becoming the centre of attention. MICA builds responsive front-end interfaces and server-side back-end functionality around a defined requirement.

When a conventional website is enough, the build stays straightforward. When the project needs a custom interface, prototype, demonstration, or web-based tool, the functionality can be explored and scoped without pretending every idea needs a giant software platform.

Performance, accessibility, responsive behaviour, and practical maintenance remain part of the technical plan.
