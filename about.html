About

Built in Ontario.
Working across Canada.

MICA Digital is an Indigenous-owned Canadian studio led by a First Nations designer, building websites for small businesses.

Why this exists

Canadian businesses need strong digital foundations.

Canada is relying on the businesses built here. When Canadian companies invest in Canadian services, more work, knowledge, and spending stays in our communities.

MICA Digital is led by a First Nations designer specializing in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), accessible design, and user-centred digital experiences. That expertise informs practical decisions about usability, interaction, and how people move through a website.

We help independent businesses establish a clear, credible online presence that supports real business goals.

Based in Barrhaven, Ottawa, MICA Digital works remotely with businesses across Ontario and Canada.

How we work

Four things stay consistent.

Clarity

Make the business understandable.

Customers should quickly know what you do and what to do next.

Character

Build around the actual business.

The structure, words, and design should reflect the work behind them.

Ownership

Full ownership from day one.

Your domain, content, and finished website belong to you. You keep the files, access, and practical control needed to manage it independently.

Support

Choose the level of help you need.

Take over the site or keep monthly support for updates and maintenance.
