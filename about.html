About

Small studio.
Serious thinking.

Neighbourhood Web is an independent Ottawa studio building clear, distinctive websites for small businesses.

Why this exists

Good local businesses deserve better websites.

The internet is full of capable people whose websites make them look uncertain, generic, or ten years behind their actual work.

We combine human-computer interaction, writing, visual design, and development to make sites that feel clear, credible, and easy to use.

We work closely, explain technical decisions in ordinary language, and build something you can genuinely own.
