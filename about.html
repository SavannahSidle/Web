About

Built in Ontario.
Working across Canada.

Made in Canada Digital is an Indigenous-owned Ontario studio building clear, distinctive websites for Canadian small businesses.

Why this exists

Canadian businesses need strong digital foundations.

Canada is relying on the businesses built here. When Canadian companies invest in Canadian services, more work, knowledge, and spending stays in our communities.

We help independent Canadian businesses build a professional online presence that is clear, credible, and ready to compete.

The studio is Indigenous-owned, based in Ontario, and available to businesses across the country.
