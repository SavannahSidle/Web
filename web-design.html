Designed around the business

A useful website starts with structure.

A small-business website should make the company easier to understand, trust, and choose. MICA plans the content hierarchy, navigation, visual system, and calls to action together so the finished site supports the way the business actually works.

The result is a custom design rather than a business name dropped into a standard layout. Pages are designed responsively for phones, tablets, and computers, with accessibility and performance considered from the beginning.

MICA is based in Barrhaven, Ottawa, and works remotely with businesses across Ontario and Canada.
